Annotations

  • Empty heading names the real before-state (a spreadsheet) so the reader can picture it. A competitor's "Import your data" would fail the swap test.
  • Error opens with the outcome the reader fears most, then lists the exact rows. Row numbers can be proven true or false, which is the point.
  • Success leads with the count and gives the next action in the same breath. The skipped-rows line is plain on purpose: a number, a reason, nothing to decode.
  • CTAs follow verb + what you get: "Choose a CSV file", "Upload corrected file". No "Submit", no "Get started".
