Landing page copy

Diagnosis: A, blank brief. Audience: owners and managers of small physiotherapy clinics. One primary action: start a free trial. Differentiator I can support from the prompt: it schedules recurring treatment plans.

Page copy

Book the whole course of treatment in one go

Tidewater is the scheduling app for physiotherapy clinics. Choose six weekly sessions with one practitioner and one room, and the calendar holds every slot.

Start my free trial

Recurring plans, booked once

Set the start date and the weekly rhythm. Tidewater books every session around each practitioner's hours and the treatment room, and shows you any clash before you confirm.

Reminders patients reply to

A text goes out 24 hours before each visit. The patient replies C to confirm or R to rebook, and your calendar updates itself.

Cancelled slots refill

When someone cancels, Tidewater texts the first patient on your waitlist. Whoever replies first gets the slot.

Annotations

  • Headline passes all three questions: the reader can picture it (a full course, one booking), it can be proven true or false, and a generic booking app can't say it. It is the page's one punchy moment.
  • Subhead names the category and audience, then makes the claim concrete with a number of sessions.
  • Blurbs each connect feature, benefit, outcome. The reply keywords (C, R) are specific enough to check.
  • Left out on purpose: stacked benefits, "no X, no Y" lists, "seamless", exclamation marks.

Claims declined

  • "Cut no-shows" in the reminders blurb. Not supported by the brief. [NEED: before/after no-show rate from pilot clinics] would unlock it, and it would make a stronger hero than anything above.
  • "Loved by physiotherapists". [NEED: customer review export or a named testimonial]
