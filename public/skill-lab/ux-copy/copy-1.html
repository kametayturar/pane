Rationale

  • Empty state follows what this is, why it's empty, how to start. The heading says why it's empty, the body says what the feature does, and the primary button is the first step.
  • Error follows what happened, why, how to fix. It gives row numbers because "some rows are invalid" sends a person hunting through 1,000 lines. The sentence "Nothing was imported" answers the first worry, which is whether the file half-loaded.
  • Success leads with the number, because that is what the user wants to check. One short consequence ("ready to book") and one honest detail (6 skipped) keep it from over-celebrating.
  • Buttons start with a verb and name the outcome: "Choose CSV file", "Upload fixed file". Neither says "Submit" or "OK".

Localization notes

  • Keep "CSV" untranslated. Use the local word for "row" that spreadsheet apps use in that market.
  • Counts need plural rules: "1 patient imported" versus "128 patients imported", including languages with more than two plural forms.
  • "Ready to book" is idiomatic; translate by meaning (can be booked now), not word for word.
  • German and Finnish buttons run about 30% longer. Leave room for "Download error report" on one line, or allow it to wrap.
