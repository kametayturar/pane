Context: top of the marketing page. User state: a clinic owner comparing scheduling tools, skimming. Tone: confident, practical. Constraints: headline under 60 characters, blurbs under 30 words.

Rationale

  • The headline states the result a clinic owner wants (a full diary) and the thing they dislike (phone calls). It avoids "streamline" and "powerful".
  • The subheadline names the product category and audience once, then lists three concrete things the product does.
  • Each blurb starts with the user's outcome, not the feature: "Patients book themselves", not "Online booking module".
  • Button copy is a verb plus an outcome. "Get started" and "Learn more" are left out on purpose.

Localization notes

  • "Phone calls" and "diary" are regional: use "calendar" or "agenda" where "diary" is unfamiliar, for example in US English.
  • "Keep your clinic full" is a figure of speech; check that it reads as "have enough patients" and not as capacity in the target language.
  • "No-shows" needs a local term: patients who miss appointments without cancelling.
