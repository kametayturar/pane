UX Copy: Welcome email after signup

Context: sent seconds after a clinic admin creates an account. User state: excited but short of time, wants to know the first step. Tone: warm and professional. Constraints: subject under 50 characters, one primary button.

Recommended copy

Subject: Your Tidewater clinic is ready
Preview text: Add your first practitioner and you can take bookings today.

Hi Priya,

Welcome to Tidewater. Your account for Harbour Physio is set up and ready for its first booking.

Three steps get you there. The first takes about 5 minutes:

  1. Add your practitioners. Enter each name and their working hours.
  2. Create appointment types. For example, "Initial assessment, 45 min".
  3. Share your booking link. Paste it into your website or email signature.
Add your first practitioner

Your patients are in a spreadsheet? Go to Settings › Import and upload it as a CSV file whenever you're ready.

Questions? Reply to this email. A person on our support team will answer within one business day.

The Tidewater team

Rationale

  • The email has one job: get a first practitioner added. The button repeats step 1 so the click matches the promise.
  • Onboarding is one concept at a time. Three numbered steps, then one optional mention of CSV import, kept below the button.
  • The tone is warm but not effusive: no exclamation marks, no "We're so excited". The admin is busy.
  • "Reply to this email" gives a human route without a link to a help centre.

Localization notes

  • Variables: {first_name}, {clinic_name}. Names may precede or follow the greeting depending on language.
  • "About 5 minutes" and "one business day" should become locale-appropriate phrases, not literal units.
  • The quoted appointment type is an example; localize the example to something common in that market.
