Set up your clinic in three steps:

  1. Click Add practitioner and enter a name and working hours.
  2. Click Appointment types and create one for each service, for example “Initial assessment, 45 minutes”.
  3. Click Share booking link and paste the link into your website or email signature.

We recommend finishing steps 1 and 2 before you share the link, so patients see real availability.
