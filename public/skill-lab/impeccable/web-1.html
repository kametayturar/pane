What the day sheet takes off the front desk.

Cancellations are filled while you work.

When a patient cancels, Tidewater texts the waiting list in order of how long they have waited and how well the slot matches their treatment length. The first yes takes it. You see a booked slot, not a phone call.

Reminders that patients actually answer.

Two texts per visit: a reply-to-confirm the day before, and an address-and-room note an hour ahead. Unconfirmed appointments are flagged on the sheet so you can ring only the ones that matter.

Rooms, tables and the pool, never double-booked.

Treatments carry what they need: a plinth, the hydro pool, a second therapist. Tidewater only offers times when every one of them is free, so a 90-minute post-op session cannot collide with the group class.
