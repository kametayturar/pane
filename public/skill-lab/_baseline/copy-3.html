Headline

Scheduling that keeps your clinic running smoothly

Subheadline

Tidewater is the easy-to-use scheduling app for small physiotherapy clinics. Fill your calendar, reduce no-shows and spend less time on admin.

Feature blurbs

A calendar that works the way you do

See every practitioner and room in one view. Drag and drop to rebook, and Tidewater warns you about clashes before they happen.

Fewer no-shows, fuller days

Automatic SMS and email reminders keep patients on track. If someone cancels, offer the slot to your waiting list in one click.

Online booking, 24/7

Give patients a simple link to book, change or cancel appointments any time. Less time on the phone, more time with patients.
