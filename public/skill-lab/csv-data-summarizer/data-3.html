Insights

  • Support load is steady except for Jul to Sep. Tickets sit between 1,190 and 1,420 in the other nine months, then reach 1,495, 1,610 and 1,702. Mean 1360.75, std 163.73.
  • First response is the metric that moved most: 33 to 49 min outside Q3, then 58, 66 and 71. The December value is 36 min, close to the median of 41.50.
  • The three metrics are strongly correlated: tickets and response 0.97, response and churn 0.99. Months with more tickets had slower replies and more lost accounts.
  • Every metric recovered by December: 1,260 tickets, 36 min, 16 churned accounts, all within the range seen in Jan to Jun.
  • Statistical outliers (1.5 x IQR rule): first_response_min in Sep (71) is the only flagged value. churned_accounts has none, although its Q3 months are the three highest.
