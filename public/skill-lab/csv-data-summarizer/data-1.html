Insights

  • All three plans grow every month, with no dips. Between January and December solo went from 410 to 590 (+43.9%), clinic from 220 to 366 (+66.4%) and group from 60 to 92 (+53.3%).
  • Clinic is the fastest-growing plan. Solo is still the largest, at 56.3% of paying accounts in December, down from 59.4% in January.
  • Total paying accounts rose from 690 to 1,048 (+51.9%). The three plan columns correlate at 0.99 or higher with each other because all trend upward, so the heatmap says little about which plan drives which.
  • Mean accounts per month: solo 502.08, clinic 295.25, group 76.33. The distributions are spread flat across their range, which is what steady growth produces.
  • Churned accounts spike in Jul, Aug and Sep (24, 29, 31 against a mean of 20.17), and the same months show the slowest monthly gains in solo, clinic and group.
