Why did churn rise in Q3?

Churn rose in Q3 as support slowed: first response went from 37 to 65 minutes while accounts lost went from 48 to 84.

  1. Accounts lost track first-response time month by month (r = 0.99 over 12 months).
  2. Ticket volume rose 26% in the same quarter, from 3,805 to 4,807, a likely reason replies slowed.
  3. Both recovered in Q4: response 49, 40, 36 min and accounts lost 22, 18, 16.
  4. The data cannot prove cause. It has no exit reasons and no churn by plan.

What does the data not show?

Association, not proofMonth by month, accounts lost and first-response time move together (r = 0.99 across 12 months). Tickets rose in the same months, so slower replies and a busier queue cannot be told apart here. The data has no exit reasons and no churn by plan.
