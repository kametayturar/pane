Strategic question

Is the support team healthy now, and which numbers should it watch to stay that way?

Recommended visualization

KPI scorecard: four cards, each with the current value, the September peak, the H1 baseline and a 12-month sparkline. Status is written out ("Below H1 average") and marked with a symbol, never colour alone. Renders as 16:9 SVG.

Data and assumptions

  • December values: first response 36 min, 1,260 tickets, 16 accounts lost; tickets per account 1,260 / 1,048 = 1.20.
  • H1 baseline (Jan to Jun): first response 39 min, 1,256 tickets, 1.61 tickets per account, 17 accounts lost. Used because no targets were provided.
  • Red marks the September peak only, as the single risk reference. No other red on the slide.

Quality check

  • Headline is one proposition. Each card shows value, comparison and trend. Number formats are consistent (thousands separators, two decimals for ratios).
  • Self-score 20 of 24. Open item: real service-level targets would replace the H1 baseline.
