Strategic question

Why did the number of accounts we lose jump in Q3, and what should the team do about it?

Recommended visualization

Small multiples on a shared month axis: accounts lost (columns) above first-response time (line), with Q3 shaded and the only navy marks. Comparison type: time series, two measures, one story. Renders as 16:9 SVG.

Data and assumptions

  • Q3 is Jul to Sep. Accounts lost: 24 + 29 + 31 = 84 against 16 + 15 + 17 = 48 in Q2 (+75%).
  • Average first response: (58 + 66 + 71) / 3 = 65 min against (37 + 33 + 41) / 3 = 37 min in Q2 (+76%). Tickets: 4,807 against 3,805 (+26%).
  • Month by month, accounts lost and first-response time move almost together (correlation 0.99 across 12 points). That fits the story, and it still does not prove cause: tickets rose in the same months and there is no exit-reason data.
  • The 45-minute working target is an assumption, not a figure from the data. H1 averaged 39 minutes.
