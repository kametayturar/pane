What it does

Fills cancelled slots

The waiting list is texted in order of wait time, filtered by treatment length and preferred days. The first reply of yes takes the slot.

Chases unconfirmed visits

Patients get a text the day before and reply to confirm. The front desk sees who has not answered and rings only those.

Books rooms with the treatment

A treatment can need a plinth, the hydro pool or a second therapist. Tidewater offers a time only when all of them are free.
