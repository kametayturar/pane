# Credits

Pane is written from scratch, but it stands on a lot of public work. Thank you.

## Libraries we learned from

- **affectUI** by Flo Hoeller – https://www.affectui.com. The idea of one self-contained file per piece with
  prefixed CSS variables, and the habit of making an agent's invisible work legible.
- **Arc UI** by Elia Kuratli – https://uiarc.dev. Everyday controls with one physical metaphor, motion that explains
  cause and effect, and real product scenes in every demo.

We studied their public pages and posts only. No code, paid content or gated source from either library is in this repo,
and pieces that resembled their catalogues too closely were left out of the public release.

## Techniques

- Liquid Glass on the web: Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and WWDC25 session 219 (the material), the refraction
  write-ups by kube.io and open-source experiments by Shu Ding and others (backdrop-filter with SVG displacement maps).
- Ordered (Bayer) dithering and halftone, after Robert Ulichney's *Digital Halftoning*.
- FLIP animation (Paul Lewis), scroll-driven animations (Chrome team docs), `@starting-style` and container queries (MDN).
- "Novelty budget" – keep most of a screen familiar and spend surprise on rare moments – from Rauno Freiberg's writing.
